## Email body

Dear Sir,

**Subject: GenService Platform — Completion of User Acceptance Testing and Request for Sign-Off**

I am pleased to inform you that User Acceptance Testing (UAT) on the **GenService Platform** has been concluded, and all issues raised by the General Service team during the testing period have been resolved and deployed to the live environment.

The platform has been tested and confirmed working across the following areas:

| Module | Capability confirmed |
|---|---|
| Request Management | Ticketed requests with structured approval routing and status tracking |
| Vehicle Maintenance Register | End-to-end tracking from request through workshop to completion, with cost capture |
| Maintenance Register | Equipment and Facility maintenance, plus a scheduler with automated reminders and escalation |
| Fuel & Power | Generator daily readings, run-hours, service countdown, fuel consumption and generator kW capture |
| Diesel Supply & Distribution | Bulk procurement with running balance and fully traceable distribution |
| Electricity & DStv | Purchase registers with running balances and automated expiry/low-balance alerts |
| Vehicle Registry & Documents | Full fleet register with statutory document expiry tracking |
| Feeding / Accommodation | Guest house log for staff on transit, with cost tracking |
| Reports & Analytics | On-demand reporting across all modules with filtering and Excel export |

I would like to highlight three outcomes of particular note:

1. **Audit compliance.** Following the recommendation from the last BSI audit, the system now captures generator kilowatt readings and computes consumption automatically, enabling proper diesel consumption analysis against actual electrical load.

2. **Controls and accountability.** Every record carries a full audit trail. Corrections to submitted entries are restricted to management and are stamped with the identity of the officer who made the change and the date it occurred.

3. **Automation of routine oversight.** Recurring obligations — fumigation, tank washing, waste disposal, generator servicing, vehicle document renewals and subscription expiries — now generate reminders and escalate automatically, removing dependence on manual follow-up.

Having satisfied the testing objectives, I consider the platform ready for **organisation-wide deployment**.

**Accordingly, I kindly request your confirmation that UAT is satisfactory and that the General Service department endorses proceeding to rollout.** With that confirmation, I intend to present the platform to the **Deputy Managing Director** for formal approval to deploy across the organisation.

I would be grateful to receive your response by **[date]**, so the approval process is not delayed. I remain available to walk you or the Director through any part of the system, or to provide a demonstration ahead of the submission.

Thank you for the commitment of your team throughout the build and testing phases.

## Before you send — checklist

- [ ] Confirm the Manager's name and the Director's name/title
- [ ] Insert a realistic response deadline (3–5 working days is reasonable)
- [ ] Decide whether to attach a one-page UAT summary or issue log as evidence
- [ ] Consider attaching 2–3 screenshots (Dashboard, Reports, Maintenance Scheduler)
- [ ] Confirm the DMD's correct title/name for the follow-up submission

## Note on the phrasing used

The email asks for **written confirmation** rather than a casual "yes". This is deliberate — when you go to the DMD, a documented endorsement from the owning department and its Director is much stronger than presenting the system on your own. It shows the business, not just IT, is asking for the rollout.
